About Me

I am a Civil (Water) Engineer, WASH Consultant and Technical Education Professional with over 15 years of progressive experience in engineering practice, technical leadership and the management and implementation of Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) infrastructure programmes. My expertise spans sustainable sanitation, water supply systems, groundwater development, and climate-resilient infrastructure, integrated water resources management, engineering design, construction supervision and quality assurance, particularly in arid and semi-arid lands (ASALs), including Turkana, Samburu and Marsabit counties. I have served in technical and leadership capacities with national and international organizations, including the State Department for TVET (LTVC College), USAID NAWIRI, ACDI/VOCA – Feed the Future (USAID), CARITAS Lodwar, CARITAS Samburu (Tree of Life), the International Rescue Committee (IRC), Solidarités International, (SI), Practical Action – ITDG-EA , VSF Belgium and Food for the Hungry International. I have contributed to development and humanitarian programmes supported by USAID/OFDA, ECHO, the European Commission, UNICEF, UK Aid, SIDA, and Charity Water among other donors. My experience includes coordinating multidisciplinary teams, providing engineering guidance, overseeing infrastructure projects, strengthening community-managed water systems and collaborating with national and county governments, development partners and local communities to improve sustainable WASH service delivery. I am registered with the Engineers Board of Kenya (EBK), a member of the Institution of Engineers of Kenya (IEK), registered with the Kenya Engineering Technicians Registration Board (KETRB) and an accredited Construction Supervisor with the National Construction Authority (NCA). My academic qualifications: Postgraduate student in Technical Education at the University of Eldoret (UoE), Master of Science in Integrated Water Resources Management from the Technical University of Kenya (TUK), a Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering from Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT), a Diploma in Water Engineering from the Kenya Water Institute (KEWI) and a Diploma in Environmental and Community Health from Mount Kenya University (MKU). I serve as Academic Registrar at Lodwar Technical and Vocational College, under the State Department for TVET also doubling up as a part-time lecturer at Turkana University College. I am committed to advancing climate-resilient infrastructure, sustainable WASH services, engineering innovation and technical skills development, with a strong focus on equitable access to safe water and sanitation for underserved communities.

I currently serve as a Technical Trainer and Academic Registrar at Lodwar Technical and Vocational College under the State Department for TVET. I also double up at the university. My professional interests include sustainable water resources management, climate-resilient WASH systems, applied research and competency-based skills development.
