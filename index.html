About Me

I am a Civil Engineer, WASH Specialist, Technical Trainer and Academic Registrar with extensive experience in water engineering, sanitation, civil infrastructure, humanitarian programming and technical and vocational education and training (TVET).

I currently serve as a Technical Trainer and Academic Registrar at Lodwar Technical and Vocational College under the State Department for TVET. I also double up at the university. My professional interests include sustainable water resources management, climate-resilient WASH systems, applied research and competency-based skills development.

Professional Experience

My professional experience spans more than 15 years in civil engineering, WASH infrastructure, humanitarian programming and technical education, particularly in arid and semi-arid environments.

  • Technical Trainer and Academic Registrar – Lodwar Technical and Vocational College.

  • WASH Specialist – ACDI/VOCA, USAID Programme, Turkana.
  • WASH and civil engineering assignments with organisations including CARITAS, IRC, Solidarités International, Practical Action, VSF-Belgium and other development partners.
