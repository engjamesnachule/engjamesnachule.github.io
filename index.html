About Me

Welcome to my professional website. I am Eng. James Nachule, a Civil Engineer, Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) Specialist, Technical Trainer and Academic Registrar at Lodwar Technical and Vocational College, Kenya.

My professional interests include sustainable water resource management, climate-resilient WASH infrastructure, civil engineering, technical education, research and community development.

Professional Experience

My professional experience spans civil engineering, humanitarian WASH programming, water infrastructure, technical training and institutional administration.

I have contributed to WASH and development initiatives through humanitarian organisations, development partners and technical institutions in Kenya.

Research and Academic Interests

My research interests include groundwater quality assessment, geospatial modelling, public health risk assessment, integrated water resources management and climate-resilient water systems.

I am particularly interested in applying GIS, spatial analysis and machine learning to address water security challenges in arid and semi-arid regions.
